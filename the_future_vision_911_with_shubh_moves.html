THE 15 GOLDEN RULES

01. Loyalty: Always stay loyal to your brothers and stand by them.
02. Respect: Respect every member, their opinions and their dignity.
03. No Taunting: Never insult or humiliate a brother.
04. One for All: A brother's problem matters to the group. Support him and hear the facts first.
05. Friendly Roasting: Jokes are welcome, but never cross someone's limits.
06. No Betrayal: Never break a brother's trust or turn against him unfairly.
07. Keep Secrets: Do not share private conversations or personal matters without permission.
08. Help Each Other: Give support whenever a brother genuinely needs help.
09. Solve Problems: Discuss disagreements calmly instead of creating fights.
10. SHAHZAD Leads the Team: This agreement belongs to SHAHZAD. SHAHZAD will lead and guide the group, help everyone stay united, and set a good example. He will lead with fairness and responsibility, while every member treats the others with respect. Disagreements should be discussed calmly.
11. Honesty: Always speak the truth and never deceive a brother.
12. Safety First: Never pressure a brother into something unsafe or harmful.
13. Apologize: If you make a mistake, accept it and say sorry.
14. No Groupism: Do not create unnecessary groups or divisions among members.
15. Brotherhood: Keep unity, friendship and mutual respect alive.
